Methodological Integrity & Transparency Notice
The ASD screening models are evaluated on a curated 80-record subset (39 Positive / 40 Negative) split into a 75% Training Set (N=59) with 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation and an Untouched 25% Stratified Holdout Test Set (N=20). All target-derived cumulative features (such as aq10_sum or result) were strictly purged from feature matrices to prevent target leakage. Note: High separability on this curated proof-of-concept subset is an artifact of sample curation and does not represent real-world clinical performance.
